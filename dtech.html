How to get a D-Tech Voucher

D-Tech vouchers are earned through our official Telegram bot. The bot uses an ad-based rewards system where users collect credits by watching ads. These credits can then be exchanged for vouchers, which you can redeem here to upgrade or extend your plan instantly.

Steps to claim:

  • 1

    Open the D-TECH Bot

    Go to our official Telegram bot using the link below.

  • 2

    Earn credits by watching ads

    Interact with the bot and complete ad-based tasks to collect credits.

  • 3

    Exchange for a Voucher

    Once you have enough credits, convert them into a 10R, 20R, or 30R voucher code.

  • 4

    Upgrade your Plan

    Return to the dashboard and enter the voucher code to instantly upgrade or extend your plan.
